A study on reasoning and deliberation


In this study, we are interested in how people form their opinions and engage in constructive discussion with others, based on a variety of topics. You should have received an invitation to participate in the study via Prolific.



Critical thinking and discussing our thoughts with others help people make better-informed decisions and contribute to society’s ability to work through collective issues.

In this study, we are particularly interested in how people think through problems, weigh different arguments, and exchange ideas with others.

Read the information letter for more details about the study.

You can contribute!

As a participant, you will be asked to:

  1. Read a short scenario and provide your opinion, including a short argumentation.
  2. Read the opinions and arguments of other participants. You may be asked to evaluate the argumentation of others.
  3. Potentially, join a short online discussion with other participants via a chat room.

This process is expected to take 20-30 minutes.

Reward

To thank participants for their time and effort, we reward them with:

  • £4.00 for completing the study (reading and writing tasks, waiting for group allocation, and completing a brief survey).
  • £1.50 (bonus) for participants assigned to a discussion group, upon completion of the discussion phase.
